## Findings rejected during verification

Recorded so the same false leads are not re-raised:

- **"Placeholder/TODO text" in 4 posts** — false positive. The case-insensitive
  scan matched substrings (`TodO`, `TKtK`, `xxx`) inside long base64 citation
  URLs and a Roman-numeral anchor (`src-xxx` = 30). No real placeholders exist.
- **"Too short" in 16 posts** — false positive. The floor used was the published
  10th percentile (2572 words), but 33 published posts fall below it and the
  shortest is 486 words. Every flagged post (2296-2563w) is inside normal
  published range, and all end on complete, conclusive sentences.
- **"Missing audio" in all 146** — not a defect. Only 38% of published posts
  have narration; the blob upload step only runs in CI.
- **Title-similarity duplicate detection** — too weak to rely on. It missed the
  kuru duplicate entirely (different vocabulary, same subject) and produced a
  false hit on `the-smell-that-preceded-diagnosis` (disease diagnosis by scent)
  versus `the-smell-that-preceded-electricity` (petrichor and ozone). Body
  content fingerprinting is the reliable method.

## Separate issue: citation link rot

Sampling citation URLs found the published corpus in worse shape than the
backlog (7/14 reachable versus 11/14). The recurring failure is
`vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/...`, an expiring Gemini
grounding redirect the generator writes directly into the sources list. These
decay into 404s over time across the whole site.

This is a pipeline defect, not a backlog defect, and it is worth fixing
separately: resolve grounding redirects to their final destination URL at
generation time instead of storing the redirect.
